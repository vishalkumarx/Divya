Divya Sharma Psychologist - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

At the website of Divya Sharma Psychologist, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website and Google Authentication for submitting reviews.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google Authentication to leave a review, we collect the following basic information from your Google Profile:

  • Your Name (to display on your review)
  • Your Email Address (for internal moderation and security)
  • Your Profile Picture (if applicable)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information exclusively to:

  • Identify you as the author of the review.
  • Prevent spam and duplicate review submissions.
  • Communicate with you regarding your review or appointment if necessary.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Your review (name and comment) will be publicly visible on our website only after it has been manually approved by our administrator.

4. Data Security

We use Supabase, a secure cloud database platform, to store and protect your data. All authentication processes are handled securely via Google OAuth.

5. Your Rights

You may request to have your review and associated data deleted at any time by contacting us directly.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at: info@divyapsychologist.com
