About Dr. Divya Sharma

Dr. Divya Sharma is a compassionate psychologist dedicated to helping individuals overcome emotional challenges and discover inner strength. She specializes in anxiety therapy, relationship counseling, stress management, and personal growth.

Her therapeutic approach combines empathy and evidence-based techniques to provide a safe environment where clients can heal and grow.
